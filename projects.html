Projects

SmartClinic

SmartClinic is a premium full-stack clinic management platform featuring an AI-powered wait-time prediction service. Built with Node.js and Python ML, it streamlines healthcare access by providing live queue monitoring, intelligent clinic discovery, and specialized medical triage dashboards.

MzansiEd

MzansiEd is an AI-powered study companion designed for the South African CAPS curriculum. It leverages Gemini AI and custom Computer Vision to provide students with instant concept explanations, 'Scan & Solve' problem guidance, and a comprehensive database of past papers.

Mentor Mentee Connect

MentorMenteeConnect is a smart platform that bridges the gap between mentors and mentees through seamless, AI-powered matching. It makes guidance accessible, meaningful, and impactful—anytime, anywhere.

MoodleTrack

MoodleTrack is a cross-platform mobile app that helps Wits University students stay on top of Moodle notifications, deadlines, and events in one place. It also supports personal event tracking with smart reminders, so nothing important slips through the cracks.

MouseMate

MouseMate turns your phone into a wireless mouse and keyboard for your computer over Wi-Fi. It uses a Flutter mobile app for controls and a Python server with pyautogui to remotely move the mouse, click, scroll, and type—just like a real input device.

HustleHub

HustleHub is a platform that connects clients with skilled workers for both short-term and long-term opportunities. Built during the Adapt IT Hackathon 2025, it empowers communities by making it easier to discover talent, create jobs, and turn skills into income.

ScholarSync

Scholar Sync is a comprehensive bursary and funding management system that streamlines student financial aid applications and funding administration. Built with C# and SQL Server, it enables efficient application tracking, donor management, and data-driven oversight for academic institutions.

Woofer-ChatOnVibes

ChatOnVibes is a real-time social networking app that lets users connect through chat, friend requests, and status updates in a vibrant mobile experience. Built with a native Android frontend and a PHP/MySQL backend, it brings seamless communication and social interaction to life.
